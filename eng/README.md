# Engineering notes

The durable rationale behind `mini`'s storage, artifact, and publishing internals — the *why* that isn't obvious from the code, written for someone (maybe you) returning to this repo cold. The feasibility studies and migration logs that used to live here are gone; this is the distilled conclusion. The skill (`.agents/skills/mi-ni/`) is the *how*; these notes are the *why*.

Start with whichever question you're holding:

- [Artifacts and the content-addressed store](./artifacts.md) — why a step returns an `Artifact` handle instead of a volume `Path`, the CAS-plus-refs layering, and how storage is scoped (the store is project-wide; the memo store and volume are per-experiment).
- [The storage backend: Hugging Face buckets](./storage-backend.md) — why a bucket, and the ~2–3s latency floor that shapes the whole batch-or-parallelize API.
- [Publishing reports to the web](./publishing.md) — why `publish` is a separate, outward-facing verb from `put`, how a report bundle reaches the web through a single `<base>` switch, and which renditions (thumbnails, provenance, the PDF) are made at export rather than in the read-only build.
- [Environments: production, dev, and the backup](./environments.md) — why the line between production and dev is publishing rather than the size of a run, why a storage profile replaces the pair and the Modal Environment and inherits the rest, why dev pins never enter `publish.lock`, and why the backup is a pull from a trust domain the development tokens cannot reach.
- [Reclaiming storage: `mini gc`](./gc.md) — the three durable planes every experiment leaves behind, and the mark-and-sweep that reclaims the CAS without ever collecting a live memo hit.
- [Reproducible GPU runs](./determinism.md) — why the same seed gave three different models, what the `XLA_FLAGS` we set bought and cost (re-measured at full length: about free), why a shared compilation cache replaced them on 2026-10-31 (one compile per program and card model, claimed through a `modal.Dict`; JAX's own key splits a card model by host pool), why the flags live in the environment rather than in Python, why a JAX bump moves the digest without moving the memo key (and how a run that straddles one is detected), and why "just tolerate small differences" doesn't survive contact with a content-addressed DAG.
- [Spending GPU time on arithmetic](./gpu-efficiency.md) — conventions for new experiments: which GPU, why jit once at module level with operators as arguments, fixed shapes, how to count compiles, why the volume commit stays, and why JAX's persistent compilation cache doesn't help on Modal.
- [Non-goals and recorded decisions](./decisions.md) — chunked datatrees, checkpoints, `obstore`, the HF cache tier, why no hosted experiment tracker, why the code fingerprint is ours rather than a library's, and the open/deferred list with issue links.
- [Operational constraints](./operations.md) — the egress allow-list, Modal gRPC TLS, CORS/Range, which progress transport each execution path uses, and why containers are left unpinned: environment facts that cause confusing failures when they're missing.
