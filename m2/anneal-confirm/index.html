Here the two moves become one. \(\tau_\ell\) falls from 0.1 at the start to 0.025 at epoch 400, smoothly on a log scale. So it is still close to 0.1 while the anchor settles, and reaches the halfway point (0.05) at epoch 200. We call this recipe τ_ℓ anneal.

This is the confirmation run for adopting τ_ℓ anneal as the recipe of record. It trains the recipe to the end at eight fresh seeds (712 to 719), with a control at each seed. It also trains it to epoch 64 at forty seeds (704 to 743), to count lost anchors.

Scope and method¶

This is an exploratory study, with no preregistration and no gate. We set four checks before the runs. They guide the choice of recipe without deciding it, so a recipe that misses one narrowly can still be the better choice:

  1. Every run at seeds 712 to 719 keeps the anchor (removalRemoval The drop on difference under the full edit at every position and slice, as a share of the way from the clean score to the target null. Higher is better, and a run below 0.5 has lost the anchor. at least 0.5).
  2. No run at those seeds ends over the spillSpill The largest drop on any other op, at the dose where it is largest. Lower is better, and a run above 0.02 is over the criterion. criterion of 0.02.
  3. Removal is no lower than that of falloff at seeds 704 to 711. The task passes the gate of the D2.2 design against the control at the same seed: a mean change over ops of at least −0.02, and at least −0.05 on any one op.
  4. At most one run of the forty loses the anchor. Discussion¶

τ_ℓ anneal meets three of the four checks outright. Every run at the fresh seeds keeps the anchor (check 1), none ends over the spillSpill The largest drop on any other op, at the dose where it is largest. Lower is better, and a run above 0.02 is over the criterion. criterion though one ends at it (check 2), and the count of lost anchors is the lowest a count of forty can give (check 4).

Check 3 has two halves, and the task half passes on every run. The late rise is still there on one seed in eight, at about the size the soft pool scoutsoft pool scout The pool over slices 1 to 3 with \(\tau_\ell\) held at 0.1 keeps the anchor on all eight seeds with no early spill, and one seed rises late to near the criterion. saw. So the slow anneal of \(\tau_\ell\) does not remove the late kind of spill; at most it keeps it at the line here.

The late spill scoutlate spill scout The late spill follows the anti anneal, and sharpening \(\tau_\ell\) to 0.025 over epochs 64 to 360 lowers it with no measured cost to removal or the task. found the effect of the late sharpening to be about the size of the difference between two GPUs, so a confirmation at eight seeds can't tell whether it helps. What the run shows is no cost we can separate from the seed draw: removal is lower than under τ_ℓ fixed on the mean, and E1 can't say whether the anneal or the seeds did that.

Two things favor the pool recipe over falloff. It chooses the slice within a range set by the architecture (slices 1 to \(L-1\)), and its early callCall The early verdict on whether a run kept the anchor, from mean α at the example answers at slice 3 over epochs 44 to 64. Its count of lost anchors, none of forty against one, is a tie at this many seeds.

Against the pool recipe, one seed in eight ends at the spill line, while the eight stored runs of falloff end at most at 0.014. That is one run, set against the seed draw and the GPU shift, so it is a small mark rather than a difference we can rely on. Under the block-1 edit it goes over on one seed, as one run of every recipe does, and its removal varies across seeds as under the step recipes.

The early bumps on two seeds are the other mark, and the larger one goes well over the line. Under τ_ℓ fixed the soft pool scout saw none at the same checkpoints, but at epoch 32 the anneal has moved \(\tau_\ell\) by about one percent, so the two recipes are nearly the same there and the difference is most likely between the two draws of seeds. Both bumps cleared within 64 epochs, and the step recipes were never scored this early, so we can't say whether they bump too.
