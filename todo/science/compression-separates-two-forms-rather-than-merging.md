# Compression separates the two forms rather than merging them

Narrowing the residual stream from 64 to 32 to 16 leaves ρ at 0 and the angles at their null, so capacity pressure buys no sharing at these scales. What it does instead is spend the remaining capacity unevenly: hex held-out accuracy runs 1.00 / 1.00 / 0.72 while named runs 0.67 / 0.43 / 0.05, and fitted precision σ coarsens 0.033 / 0.068 / 0.257. That asymmetry follows from the snap-margin account — a hex answer's runner-up is a whole grid step away, so a coarse read still lands on the right digit, while an irregular 140-name palette needs six times the resolution. So "which form survives compression" is a property of the answer vocabulary's spacing, and a capacity-pressure design that wants both forms to feel the squeeze equally has to match their margins.
