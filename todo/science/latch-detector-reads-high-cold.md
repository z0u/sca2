# The latch detector reads high at cold τ

All ten of ex-2.1.11's survey vetoes sit at τ ≤ 0.12, and seven have healthy pulls with π 0.52–0.84 — inside what ex-2.1.9's calibration measured as an empty gap (latched ≥ 0.85, healthy ≤ 0.03, calibrated near τ = 0.1 pooling). Cold pooling concentrates softmin weight, so a modest syntax-role alignment reads as a large π. Harmless in ex-2.1.11 (the cold edge scores low on m_line anyway), but any experiment that wants to sample τ ≲ 0.1 needs the detector recalibrated at matching τ first.
