This report trains the recipe at eight seeds under eight anti weights, with a control at each seed: 72 runs, in two rounds. The first round held 0.115 or 0.2 at every slice. The second added constant weights of 0.14 and 0.17 between them, and an anneal to 0.115 from a lower start of 0.17.

The two at 0.2 miss it on removal. flat 0.115, level 0.115 and level 0.14 miss it by one run over the spill criterion each, which is too few to tell them from the recipe.

Where the anchor sits (E4)¶

The falloff was chosen by trial, high at the embedding and low at slice 4. Does its shape follow where the op and the other ops sit on e₁?

The op sits mostly at slices 2 to 4, peaking at slice 3, and hardly at all on the embedding. The other ops lean on e₁ most at slice 1, at about half of what the op has there, and less with depth. Every condition has the same profile to within a few hundredths, so the falloff did not make it.

The falloff is high where the other ops lean and low where the op sits: it pushes other ops off e₁ hardest at the embedding and slice 1, and least at slices 3 and 4, where the op sits. A weight the same at every slice has to do both jobs with one number, which may be why it works only in a narrow band.
