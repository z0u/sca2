Every run keeps the anchor. Removal under τ_ℓ fixed is a little above that of τ_ℓ anneal on the seed mean, and above falloff too. It spreads more than it did under the same recipe at seeds 704 to 711, though less than under the anneal.

Seed by seed, the differences run both ways, by up to 0.11: τ_ℓ fixed removes more on four of the eight seeds. The two low seeds of the anneal confirmation (719 and 718) remove more here, but seed 715, which removed well under the anneal, is the lowest here, and seed 719 is among the lowest under both.

So neither account from the anneal confirmation fits on its own. The low seeds there were not low because of the seed alone, since seed 718 removes about as much as the seed mean here. Nor does the anneal lower removal across the board, since it removes more on some seeds.

By epoch 400 the anneal has taken \(\tau_\ell\) down fourfold, so the end gaps could still be a late effect of the anneal that differs by seed. Removal along training shows when the gaps open.

The runs of a seed part ways early. At epoch 32, where the anneal has moved \(\tau_\ell\) by under one percent (0.0994 against 0.1), the two runs of some seeds are up to 0.43 apart in removal, and most pairs come back together by about epoch 160.

The two largest end gaps (seeds 718 and 719, a tenth each) are set by epoch 64 to 96, when \(\tau_\ell\) under the anneal is still within about a tenth of 0.1 (0.088 at epoch 96). They change little afterward, while the anneal does most of its work. The smaller lasting gaps, on seeds 714 and 715, wander by a few hundredths along the way.

So the end gaps were set while the two recipes were nearly the same. That fits a picture in which the final removal of a run is sensitive to small changes early on, while the anchor settles. If so, eight seeds of either recipe give a noisy estimate of its mean.

This does not rule out that a slightly sharper \(\tau_\ell\) at epochs 64 to 96 matters, but a difference that small would be hard to tell from the same sensitivity. Seed 719, low under both, may also be a hard seed for the pool.

Seven runs pass the task gate, with the mean over ops within about a hundredth of the control. Seed 718 misses it: its mean over ops is −0.028, and two ops, value-hsv and sat-hsv, end −0.07 and −0.06 below the control.

This run learns those two ops late: at epoch 160 its clean score on value-hsv is 0.22, where the other seven runs are at 0.33 or more, and it is still rising at the end. Under the anneal, the same seed learned them on time, and another seed (717) was the late one but caught up. So this too looks like a difference between runs more than a property of the recipe. falloff also has one run of eight that misses the gate; step, soft τ, τ_ℓ anneal, and τ_ℓ fixed at 704 to 711 have none.

Discussion¶

τ_ℓ fixed meets three of the four checks outright: every run at the fresh seeds keeps the anchor (check 1), none ends near the spillSpill The largest drop on any other op, at the dose where it is largest. Check 3 has two halves. The removalRemoval The drop on difference under the full edit at every position and slice, as a share of the way from the clean score to the target null. Higher is better, and a run below 0.5 has lost the anchor. half passes: removal is above that of falloff on the seed mean. The task half misses by one run of eight, on a seed that learns two ops late, as falloff also misses by one (E1).

We take the two recipes as removing about the same, since the difference in the seed mean between them is within the differences seen at single seeds.

The pairing with τ_ℓ anneal finds that the uneven removal came mostly from neither the anneal nor the seeds: the low runs move between seeds when the recipe changes only a little. So the spread of removal across eight runs is partly how sensitive a run is to small changes, and a seed mean over eight runs carries that noise.

The same holds for the other problems seen at eight seeds: the late rise in spill, the early bumps, and the late learner each appeared under one recipe at a seed and not under the other. A count over more runs, as in E3, is the steadier measure for these.

Between the two, τ_ℓ fixed is simpler, and nothing in this confirmation suggests the anneal improves on it. Here the late rise appeared under the anneal and not under the fixed recipe.

We adopt τ_ℓ fixed as the recipe of record for D2.2, and the next step is to run the full scoring suite on it.
